Government of Canada program

The Canadian Dental Care Plan.

If you are covered by the CDCP, tell us when you book. We will check how the plan applies to your visit and explain any amount you may need to pay before treatment begins.

Confirm Select Dental accepts CDCP patients before launch

For residents without dental insurance
A federal program for Canadian residents who do not have access to dental insurance.
Income-based
Eligibility includes an adjusted family net income below $90,000, among other criteria.
Part or all of the fee
Depending on income, the plan covers part or all of its established fee for eligible services.

Eligibility and coverage are decided by the Government of Canada, not the clinic. Co-payments may apply.
