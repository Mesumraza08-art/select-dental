What is the CDCP?

The Canadian Dental Care Plan is a federal program that helps eligible Canadian residents who do not have access to dental insurance pay for oral health care. Health Canada oversees the plan, and Sun Life administers it on behalf of the Government of Canada.

Enrolled members receive a member card from Sun Life. Participating dental offices bill the plan directly for covered services.

Who may be eligible

In general, to qualify you must:

  • Be a Canadian resident for tax purposes
  • Not have access to dental insurance, for example through an employer, pension or a family member's plan
  • Have an adjusted family net income under $90,000
  • Have filed your tax return for the previous year (and your spouse or common-law partner, if you have one)

These are general criteria. The Government of Canada decides eligibility, and the rules can change. Check the official criteria.

How coverage and payment work

The CDCP pays a share of its established fee for each covered service. Your share, called a co-payment, depends on your adjusted family net income:

CDCP co-payment by adjusted family net income
Adjusted family net incomeYour co-payment
Under $70,000No co-payment
$70,000 to $79,99940% of the CDCP established fee
$80,000 to $89,99960% of the CDCP established fee
  • If the clinic's fee for a service is higher than the CDCP established fee, you may pay the difference. We will tell you before treatment.
  • Some services need approval from the plan before treatment (preauthorization). We will let you know if this applies.
  • Services the plan does not cover are your responsibility, and we will always discuss them with you first.
