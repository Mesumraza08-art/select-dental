04 /New patients

Your first visit, made simple.

  1. 01

    Book

    Request a time online or call us. Tell us if it is your first visit, if anything is bothering you and whether you have insurance or CDCP.

  2. 02

    Visit

    We start with a conversation about your health and any worries, then a thorough, gentle exam with any x-rays you need.

  3. 03

    Care plan

    We explain what we found in plain language, walk through options and costs, and plan next steps together. No pressure.

What to bring

  • Photo ID
  • Your dental insurance card or plan details, if you have coverage
  • Your CDCP member information, if you are enrolled
  • A list of the medications and supplements you take
  • Previous dental records or x-rays, if you can easily get them
  • Any questions or concerns you want to talk about
